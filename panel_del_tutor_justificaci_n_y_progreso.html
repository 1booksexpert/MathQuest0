Pedagogical Justification

The current module focuses on solidifying foundational numerical cognition through exercises designed to strengthen Subitizing and Cardinality.

visibility Conceptual Subitizing

Activities require rapid recognition of clustered quantities (dots, objects) without conscious counting, essential for future arithmetic fluency.

numbers Cardinal Principle

Tasks reinforce understanding that the final number counted represents the total set size, shifting focus from rote sequence to set magnitude.
