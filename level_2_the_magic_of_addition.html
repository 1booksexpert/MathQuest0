Origin Story

The plus sign is an abbreviation of the Latin word "et", which means "and". Over centuries, hurried writers simplified the letters 'e' and 't' into the cross shape we use today!

group_add

How to use it

When you want to combine groups, add things together, or grow a number larger, the plus sign is your tool. It brings things together to make a sum.
